# 67VERSE First Playable

## Experience

The player feels invited and capable because one visible route takes them from a lively social park into a readable, fast race and back to the same home.

## Player Route

1. Enter directly into Skypark with the Play gate visible.
2. Move along the gold spine or use the primary Play command.
3. Enter Skyway Sprint.
4. Race a short course against live peers when a room is available.
5. Fill empty seats with deterministic vinyl rivals.
6. Finish, replay, or return to Skypark.

## Verbs

- Move: camera-relative keyboard, touch stick, or gamepad stick.
- Jump: Space, touch Jump, or gamepad A.
- Grab/enter: E or Shift, touch Grab, or gamepad B/RT.

## Course Beats

- Start deck: safe runway and readable finish sightline.
- Sweep plaza: one moving hazard tests timing.
- Gate run: one lateral obstacle tests route choice.
- Step bridge: three forgiving pads test jump timing.
- Final sprint: a clean finish moment and placement result.

## Multiplayer

- Real-time room contract, eight seats maximum.
- The client submits bounded input commands, never positions or scores.
- The server validates message shape, size, rate, and command ranges.
- A connection timeout falls back to four local rivals using the same fixed-step player simulation.

## Accessibility

- Keyboard, touch, and gamepad share one command model.
- Reduced motion and lower quality settings remain available.
- Critical state uses text, shape, and color rather than color alone.

## Style Formula

Smooth designer-vinyl 3D with soft toy-photography shading and restrained material detail. Rounded, chunky silhouettes, broad beveled park forms, and no sharp edges or borrowed motifs. Environment uses warm starlight concrete, sage planting, terracotta and graphite structure; characters stay cool silver with Apple-yellow accents, while portals and hazards use distinct plum and coral signals. Calm sunny city-park mood, broad diffuse light, soft contact shadows, high contrast, clean readable silhouettes, consistent third-person perspective, and mobile-first legibility.
